## Ecwid Product Shelf APIs

A simple FastAPI service that fetches orders and products from Ecwid and exposes multiple endpoints to view products based on their order status.

### Features

* Products that are on shelf (ordered atleast once)
* Products that are not on shelf (never ordered)
* Products ordered at least **k** times
* Products ordered at most **k** times

### Tech Stack

* Python
* FastAPI
* Requests
* Ecwid REST APIs
